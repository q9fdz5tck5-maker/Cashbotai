Straight Answers

The questions you are already asking

Do I need a graphics card?
No. It auto-detects CUDA or Apple silicon and falls back to your processor. CPU works — it is just slower, so start with a short deck.
Does my voice sample leave my machine?
No. The model runs locally, and samples are excluded from anything you redistribute.
Can I use it for more than one webinar?
Yes. Copy the config, change the words. No per-render fee, no cap, no seat count.
Does it take payments or send email?
No to both. Buy buttons are plain links to your checkout, and opt-ins land in a file for you to export.
What about webinars I already built by hand?
The importer converts them. Narration carries across verbatim and it lists anything it could not classify.
