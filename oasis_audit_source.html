Pillar 1

Website & SEO audit

The Shopify site is live, secure, and mobile-capable — the foundation is fine. The problem: it gives Google almost nothing to rank.

S1 — Only 3 pages indexed CRITICAL

Google's index contains just the homepage, /collections/all, and /cart. There are no service pages (facials, peels, cryotherapy), no about page, no location page, no blog. Google cannot rank pages that do not exist.

S2 — Homepage title tag carries no keywords SERIOUS

The indexed title is simply "Oasis Beauty Lounge." It should communicate service + city, e.g. "Oasis Beauty Lounge | Advanced Facials in Old Town Scottsdale, AZ." This one line is the strongest single ranking signal on the site.

S3 — Cart page is indexed SERIOUS

/cart appearing in Google indicates index-hygiene gaps (missing noindex on utility pages). One-third of your indexed footprint is a shopping cart — wasted crawl equity and a poor searcher experience.

S4 — Site blocks non-browser agents (HTTP 403) SERIOUS

The site returned 403 Forbidden to every automated fetcher we used. Google renders it, but aggressive bot protection can block SEO tools, link previews, and the AI search crawlers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) that "best facial near me" queries increasingly flow through. Review Shopify bot-protection settings.

S5 — No authority signals ISSUE

No backlinks from local press, directories, or partners were found — even easy local links (Old Town Scottsdale directory, chamber, suite-mates, CareCredit link-back) are missing.

S6 — Unverified technical items NEEDS ACCESS

Schema markup, Core Web Vitals, sitemap submission, Search Console and GA4 could not be checked from outside — 30-minute verifications once access is granted.

G2 — Zero Google reviews CRITICAL

Competing facial studios in Scottsdale carry 20–120+ reviews. At zero, you lose ties on every "near me" comparison regardless of how good the service is.

Methodology, sources & scope

How this audit was performed

All findings come from publicly observable data collected July 19, 2026: search-engine indexes and snippets, public directory listings, and platform searches. No website admin, analytics, ad accounts, or private data were accessed. The website itself returns HTTP 403 to automated fetchers (noted as finding S4), so on-page checks were reconstructed from search-index data; items that require inside access are marked TBD rather than guessed. The Google Ads spend figure (~$500/day) was reported by the business and is treated as owner-provided, not independently verified — the API audit on p. 3 is how it becomes measured fact. Ad-waste percentages cited are typical ranges from comparable local-service accounts, never a claim about this account before the audit runs.

How scores are calculated

Each pillar is scored 0–10 against a standard local-business checklist (presence, completeness, consistency, activity, authority). Scores are directional diagnostics for prioritization — not industry-benchmarked metrics. Zero reviews, zero social profiles, and 3 indexed pages are verified counts; "unverified" means the item could not be confirmed to exist from public search, which itself indicates a visibility problem.
