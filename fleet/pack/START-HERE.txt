WHAT THIS IS

  A way to put several computers to work for you at once, and
  to tell them what to do by typing in plain English.

  You end up with one MAIN computer and any number of HELPER
  computers. You talk to the main one. The helpers do the work
  -- making videos, speaking words out loud, writing and
  sending email, drawing pictures.

  The helpers can be rented computers, or an old desktop in
  your closet, or both at the same time.

  There are three steps. Each one is a single line you paste.


WHAT YOU NEED

  1. One computer with a web address that points at it.
     This is the main one. A cheap rented computer is fine.

  2. Any number of other computers. These need nothing
     special. They do not need a web address. They do not
     need you to change anything on your internet box at home.

  If you do not have either yet, open GET-COMPUTERS.txt. It
  walks through buying them and pointing a web address at one.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 -- SET UP THE MAIN COMPUTER
----------------------------------------------------------------

  On the main computer, type this:

      sudo bash setup.sh

  It asks you two things and then does the rest. When it
  finishes it saves two files you will want:

      MY-FLEET-DETAILS.txt   your codes
      CONNECT-CLAUDE.txt     how to hook Claude up to this

  and it shows you one line to copy.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2 -- ADD A HELPER
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Go to another computer. Unpack this same zip there. Paste
  the line step 1 gave you.

  Repeat for every computer you want to add. Same line every
  time.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 3 -- TELL IT WHAT TO DO
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Open USE-WITH-CLAUDE.txt.

  It sets up Claude on your main computer so you can type

      make me a two minute video explaining what my shop does

  and get one back. That page is short and it is the reason
  all of this exists. Do not skip it.

  There is no step 4.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO TELL IT WORKED
----------------------------------------------------------------

  On the main computer, type:

      fleet status

  Every computer you added should be in the list, saying
  "online". If one is missing, wait half a minute and look
  again -- they check in every thirty seconds.

  If something is wrong, type this instead:

      fleet preflight

  It tells you which of the three usual problems you have,
  in words, instead of making you guess.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING
----------------------------------------------------------------

  You do not have to leave anything open. Once set up, each
  computer works quietly in the background, and starts itself
  again if the computer is restarted or loses power.

  Nothing connects IN to your helper computers. They reach out
  to the main one. That is why there is nothing to open up or
  forward, and why a computer on your home network works just
  as well as a rented one.

  Your codes are in MY-FLEET-DETAILS.txt on the main computer.
  Treat that file like a password. Anyone who has it can send
  work to your computers.

  Nothing in this zip is tied to anybody else. There are no
  codes in it and no list of somebody else's machines. The
  fleet you build is yours alone.
