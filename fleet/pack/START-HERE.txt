WHAT THIS IS

  A way to put several computers to work for you at once.

  You end up with one MAIN computer and any number of HELPER
  computers. You talk to the main one. The helpers do the work --
  making videos, speaking words out loud, writing and sending
  email, drawing pictures.

  The helpers can be rented computers, or an old desktop sitting
  in your closet, or both at the same time.


WHAT YOU NEED

  1. One computer with a web address that points at it.
     This is the main one. A cheap rented computer is fine.

  2. Any number of other computers. These need nothing special.
     They do not need a web address. They do not need you to
     change anything on your internet box at home.


HOW TO SET IT UP

  Step 1 -- on the MAIN computer, type this:

      sudo bash setup.sh

  It asks you two things and then does the rest. When it
  finishes it saves a file called MY-FLEET-DETAILS.txt and
  shows you one line to copy.

  Step 2 -- on each HELPER computer, paste that line.

  That is the whole setup. Repeat step 2 for every computer you
  want to add. There is no step 3.


HOW TO TELL IT WORKED

  On the main computer, type:

      fleet status

  Every computer you added should be in the list, saying
  "online". If one is missing, wait half a minute and look
  again -- they check in every thirty seconds.

  If something is wrong, type this instead:

      fleet preflight

  It tells you which of the three usual problems you have,
  in words, instead of making you guess.


A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

  You do not have to leave anything open. Once set up, each
  computer works quietly in the background, and starts itself
  again if the computer is restarted or loses power.

  Nothing connects IN to your helper computers. They reach out
  to the main one. That is why there is nothing to open up or
  forward, and why a computer on your home network works just
  as well as a rented one.

  Your codes are in MY-FLEET-DETAILS.txt on the main computer.
  Treat that file like a password. Anyone who has it can send
  work to your computers.
