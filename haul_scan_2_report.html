📋 Scan prompt — version 4 (draft, awaiting your go)

This is the criteria stack every future video/photo/listing scan will run. v1–3 = resale comps → urgency signals → local-season demand. v4 adds the hidden-value layer.

v4 scan procedure

  1. Identify every item; brand & model where legible — zoom frames, read labels/stamps.
  2. Resale comp each: current eBay sold / FB local price vs likely tag. Rank by spread and sell-through speed.
  3. Urgency & season: weight items the Phoenix market wants this month (July = cooling, ice baths, monsoon dehumidifiers; flag off-season capital traps).
  4. NEW — Melt/scrap floor: for anything metal, estimate material value (sterling/gold marks, brass/copper magnet test, copper cookware). Flag any item where melt > tag price as an auto-buy; state melt as the floor vs resale.
  5. NEW — Collector/misprint sweep: check every item class for the collectible variant of itself — pro-line stamps, first editions, error coins/cards, uranium glass, discontinued patterns (Pyrex, Fiestaware, MacKenzie-Childs), vintage date codes, CIB games, promo/misprint apparel & vinyl.
  6. NEW — Look inside: cases, boxes, bags, and pockets get opened — contents are unpriced value (case contents, manuals that complete a CIB set, balls in bags).
  7. Output: ranked BUY-NOW / CHECK-&-BUY / SKIP with the exact in-store verification step for each, tag-price ceiling to pay, and expected net after fees.
